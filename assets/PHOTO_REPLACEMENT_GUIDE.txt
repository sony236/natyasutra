SONIA NATYASUTRA — IMAGE-FIRST WEBSITE

This website intentionally has NO photo-card components. Photographs are normal <img> elements so you can replace them easily.

HOW TO REPLACE ANY IMAGE
1. Put your photo inside the assets folder.
2. Open the relevant HTML file.
3. Find the <img ...> for that section.
4. Change only the src value.
   Example: src="assets/my-photo.jpg"
5. Save and refresh the page.

HOME VIDEO
- assets/dance-hero.mp4 is the full-screen blended hero video.
- Replace it with your own 16:9 MP4 using the same filename.
- The video automatically blends into the dark vibrant maroon/gold theme.
